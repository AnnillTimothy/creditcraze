Privacy & cookies

Credit Compass is committed to transparent, consent-aware data practices. We use essential cookies to keep the site secure and functioning, and optional analytics or personalisation cookies only when you allow them.

What we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as messages you submit, plus technical information like browser type, approximate location, device data, and usage events. When Compass AI is used, prompts are processed to generate responses and improve the user journey where permitted.

Cookie categories

  • Essential: session integrity, security, load balancing, and consent storage.
  • Analytics: aggregated page usage, content performance, and comparison journey insights.
  • Personalisation: remembering optional preferences and improving guidance continuity.

How consent works

The banner and settings panel let you accept all cookies, keep only essential cookies, or customise optional categories. Your preference is stored locally in your browser and can be changed anytime by clearing site storage.

SEO and content optimisation

We use structured metadata, route-aware descriptions, and consent-safe analytics to understand which content helps people most. Optimisation is focused on relevance, clarity, and compliance rather than hidden tracking.
